## 1. ONE-Liner

AI-powered job matching and application automation for Indian job seekers.

## 2. Core Promise

* Discover relevant jobs across multiple job portals and company websites.

* Match candidate profiles against job descriptions and calculate an ATS compatibility score.

* Automatically apply to eligible jobs or email candidates when manual action is required.

## 3. Target user

Rahul Sharma — an Indian IT professional with 2–5 years of experience, actively seeking a job switch and applying to 20–30 jobs daily.

## 6. Tech Stack

* Frontend: Next.js + Tailwind CSS

* Backend: Python + FastAPI + Celery

* DB: PostgreSQL + pgvector

* AI: OpenAI API for resume/JD extraction, skill matching, and scoring

* Deploy: Vercel + Railway

## 7. Time-To-MVP Estimate

4–6 weeks for a solo developer using AI-assisted development.

* Week 1: Authentication, resume upload, profile extraction.

* Week 2: Job ingestion and JD extraction.

* Week 3: Matching engine and ATS scoring.

* Week 4: Email alerts, dashboard, and application tracker.

* Weeks 5–6: Supported application automation, testing, and deployment.

## 8. The One Feature: we cannot ship without (your verdict)

Reliable Job-to-Candidate Matching with Explainable ATS Scoring.

The product must accurately identify relevant jobs and explain why a candidate matches before triggering an application.

Critical constraint: Do not make unrestricted auto-application across Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, or other portals a launch dependency. Access restrictions, anti-bot controls, CAPTCHAs, and portal-specific application flows can prevent reliable automation. Start with permitted job sources and supported application workflows; provide one-click application links and email alerts wherever automated submission is unavailable.
